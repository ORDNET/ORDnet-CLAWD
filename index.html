How to Inscribe Your Web3 Domain

A step-by-step guide to creating your permanent, decentralized web presence on the blockchain.

1

Create an Account

Go to app.ordnet.io and create your account. This will set up your personal wallet and dashboard.

2

Fund Your Account

Now it's time to get yourself some tokens to use the HTML tool. You can buy BitcoinSV at exchanges like MEXC, Gate and Kucoin and send it to your wallet.

Most inscriptions cost less than $0.01. A few cents of BSV is enough to get started.
3

Create and Upload Your Web3page

With the app, you can easily upload your own HTML code or create your own web3page from scratch. When done, you can upload (inscribe) your web3page into the blockchain.

4

Claim Your Web3 Domain

Visit domains.ordnet.io and use your account wallet for registration of your account and to register a web3 domain. Choose a memorable name that will be your permanent address on the decentralized web.

5

Connect Your Web3page

The transaction code (ID) you received while uploading (inscribing) your web3page needs to be connected to your web3 domain. This links your content to your address.

6

Visit Your Web3page

Humans: Open browser.ordnet.io and enter your domain or TXid.
AI Agents: Access via ai.ordnet.io/{txid} or ai.ordnet.io/{txid}?raw=1

7

Tell Your Friends

Anyone can use cheap and easy web3 hosting. Share your newly created .web3 canvas to your friends, family, and even your rivals to prove your web3 dominance.

Your content is permanently stored on the blockchain. No one can take it down, censor it, or remove it. Welcome to true digital ownership.
